# Daily India Stock Picks - 2026-05-22
**Analyst:** Claude (claude-sonnet-4-6) | **Exchange:** NSE & BSE | **Framework:** Growth + Quality + Valuation

## Market Context
Indian equities are recovering after an 18-month broad correction, with the Nifty 50 finding support as the RBI rate-cut cycle (100+ bps expected over FY26-27) improves credit conditions; more than 80% of listed companies above ₹1,000 Cr market cap remain 20%+ below their all-time highs, creating a selective value opportunity. Institutional investors including Goldman Sachs, HSBC, and Morgan Stanley have turned constructive on India for 2026, citing a self-help earnings recovery, strong Budget capital outlay, and a broadening digital economy.

### #1 - HDFCBANK (NSE): HDFC Bank Ltd
**Sector:** Banking / Financials | **Cap:** Large Cap (INR 11,68,659 Cr) | **Conviction:** HIGH

**Why undervalued now:** HDFC Bank trades at a P/E of 15.4x — a steep 35–40% discount to its own 10-year historical average of 22–25x — as the market penalises post-merger NIM compression that is now bottoming. With HDFC Ltd fully absorbed and synergies in deposits + distribution beginning to show in margins, this is the widest valuation gap in a decade for India's largest private bank.

**Future potential:** Loan growth is returning to 14%+ YoY; FY27 is shaping up as a re-rating year as NIMs normalise, credit costs remain benign, and the liability franchise (9,455 branches) begins to monetise fully. The bank has zero promoter pledging and a pristine balance sheet.

**Key risk:** Prolonged NIM recovery delay or a macro-driven credit-quality deterioration could push re-rating further out. No promoter holding means no alignment signal, though institutional ownership of 85%+ provides stability.

### #2 - INFY (NSE): Infosys Ltd
**Sector:** Technology / IT Services | **Cap:** Large Cap (INR 4,63,351 Cr) | **Conviction:** HIGH

**Why undervalued now:** Infosys trades at P/E of 15.4x — a level last seen during the 2020 pandemic crash — despite ROCE of 41% and ROE of 29%. The market has over-penalised the sector for 2024–25 demand softness. A 4.06% dividend yield provides strong downside support while client discretionary spend is returning.

**Future potential:** AI integration (Infosys Topaz + new deal wins in digital modernisation) is accelerating deal flow. Acquisition of Optimum Healthcare IT (May 2026) adds healthcare vertical depth. Infosys has zero debt, returns cash via dividends and buybacks, and its co-founder promoter alignment (14.55% stake) keeps capital discipline high.

**Key risk:** Prolonged macro weakness in the US/EU — Infosys's primary revenue geographies — could suppress discretionary IT spending and constrain revenue growth to single digits.

### #3 - HAL (NSE): Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd
**Sector:** Industrials / Defence | **Cap:** Large Cap (INR 2,92,207 Cr) | **Conviction:** HIGH

**Why undervalued now:** HAL's P/E of 32.6x appears high in isolation, but fails to capture a ₹1.65 lakh crore+ order book providing 7+ years of revenue visibility. Budget FY2026 set a record defence capital outlay, and HAL's manufacturing order pipeline includes 156 LCH helicopters, 12 Su-30MKI aircraft, and 240 AL-31FP engines — each with multi-year execution tails.

**Key risk:** Execution delays in complex manufacturing programmes and supply-chain import dependencies (especially aero-engines) are the primary risks; government procurement cycles can be lumpy.
