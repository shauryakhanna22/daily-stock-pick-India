# Daily India Stock Picks - 2026-05-30
**Analyst:** Claude (claude-sonnet-4-6) | **Exchange:** NSE & BSE | **Framework:** Growth + Quality + Valuation

## Market Context
The Nifty 50 shed ~5% in FY2026 amid $19.7 bn in FII outflows driven by a stronger dollar, elevated crude prices, and global uncertainty — pulling quality large-caps to multi-year valuation lows, with HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank now trading at P/E multiples last seen during the 2020 COVID crash. Against this backdrop India's structural story is firmly intact: Budget 2026 allocated a record INR 6.8 lakh crore to infrastructure and defence capex, the RBI has commenced a rate-easing cycle, and Nifty-50 earnings for FY27 are forecast to grow 14–16%, making the current dislocation a compelling long-term entry window across Banking, IT, Defence, and FMCG.

### #1 - HDFCBANK (NSE): HDFC Bank Ltd
**Sector:** Banking & Financial Services | **Cap:** Large Cap (INR 11,68,659 Cr) | **Conviction:** HIGH

**Why undervalued now:** HDFC Bank trades at P/E 15.4x — its cheapest valuation in over a decade — as the market continues to penalise it for temporary NIM compression following the 2023 merger with HDFC Ltd. The integration is now complete, NIMs are recovering, and the loan-to-deposit ratio is normalising, setting up a powerful multiple re-rating. FII ownership of 44% means any return of foreign flows becomes an immediate catalyst.

**Future potential:** Management guidance points to 15–17% credit growth in FY27, powered by retail and MSME lending. ROE is on track to reach 17–18% by FY28 as leverage normalises post-merger. A re-rating from 15x to just 20x P/E (still well below the 25–30x historical range) would imply ~33% price appreciation before any earnings growth is factored in.

**Key risk:** Sustained CASA attrition if deposit rates remain elevated; any systemic NPA event in unsecured retail lending.
