## Market Context
Indian markets remain in a consolidation phase in May 2026, with the Nifty 50 down ~10% year-to-date amid $21 billion in FII outflows; however, domestic SIP flows of ₹321 billion per month continue to provide a structural bid, and mid/small-cap indices have staged a sharp recovery (Nifty Microcap 250 +21.55% in April alone). Budget 2026's record defence capex allocation and continued infrastructure push are creating selective large-cap opportunities, while several quality compounders across IT, banking, and pharma are trading near decade-low valuations — offering a compelling entry for patient investors.

**Why undervalued now:** HDFC Bank is trading at a P/E of just 15.37x — a near-decade low — and at 2.09x P/BV versus its historical range of 3.5–4x. **Key risk:** Prolonged NIM compression if deposit costs remain elevated; any macro-driven credit quality deterioration.

### #3 - CANFINHOME (NSE): Can Fin Homes Ltd
**Sector:** Housing Finance (NBFC) | **Cap:** Mid Cap (INR 11,136 Cr) | **Conviction:** HIGH

**Why undervalued now:** Can Fin Homes is trading at a P/E of just 10.26x — one of the cheapest valuations in the housing finance space — while generating an ROE of 19.73% with zero promoter pledging. Canara Bank (a Navratna PSU) holds a ~30% stake, providing implicit government backing. Affordable housing demand in India is structural and decades-long, and Can Fin operates in that sweet spot with a tight credit culture and low NPAs.

**Future potential:** India's housing finance market is expected to double over the next 5–7 years driven by urbanisation, the PMAY scheme, and RBI rate cuts stimulating mortgage demand. Can Fin's loan book growth should re-accelerate, and the stock warrants a 1.5–2x P/BV re-rating (currently <1.5x) as earnings momentum returns.

**Key risk:** Rising NPA cycle if real estate stress surfaces; margin compression if RBI rate cuts narrow spreads faster than expected.
