## Market Context
Indian equities are experiencing a selective consolidation in May 2026 as the Nifty 50 trades near 24,500, broadly flat year-to-date but with significant divergence at the sector level — defence, healthcare, and quality financials outperforming while FMCG and IT lag on rupee pressure and demand concerns. The macro backdrop remains constructive: RBI's rate-easing cycle has begun, Budget 2026's record ₹11.1 lakh crore capex push is feeding into order books, and FII flows have stabilised, creating a compelling window to accumulate quality businesses trading well below their intrinsic value.
