## Market Context

India's equity markets are at an inflection point in May 2026: the Nifty 50 is recovering from a 10%+ YTD correction while the broader market (Microcap 250 +21.5%, Smallcap 100 +18.4% in April alone) signals surging domestic conviction, powered by SIP inflows of ₹321 billion/month. The RBI's rate-cut cycle and Budget 2026's record ₹11.1 lakh crore capex push are re-rating quality franchises that had been unjustly sold off alongside FII outflows of $21 billion — today's picks target exactly that dislocation, with a focus on companies where fundamental value diverges most sharply from price.
