# Daily India Stock Picks - 2026-05-26

## Market Context
Indian equities have rebounded strongly in May 2026, with the Nifty 50 recovering from its early-2026 correction driven by stabilising FII flows, RBI rate cut expectations, and robust domestic institutional buying across defence, infrastructure, and consumption themes. Nifty midcaps and smallcaps continue to trade at elevated absolute P/E multiples (~36x SMID median) but over 80% of listed companies above ₹1,000 Cr remain 20–30% below all-time highs, creating selective bottom-up opportunities for patient investors.

**Key risk:** Prolonged NIM compression from high-cost deposits inherited from legacy HDFC Ltd; any further FII selling pressure given the 44% FII holding.

### #9 - COALINDIA (NSE): Coal India Ltd
